```text
Block one is Find. Start by checking your campaigns. Is there still budget left? Has the rate changed? Did the creator update the rules? Block two is Edit. Out of all the clips OpusClip gives you, pick the best three to five. Run the checklist on each one, and skip anything that fails. ```text
```
```text
Block three is Post. One clip goes to three platforms: YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Space them out, two to three hours apart, instead of posting everywhere at once. Keep your titles under sixty characters. Use two to four hashtags, not twenty. And if you're clipping someone else's content, credit the creator in the caption. A simple posting schedule makes this easy, so you're never guessing what goes out next. One more thing. TikTok's Creator Rewards needs ten thousand followers and original videos over one minute, and Instagram's creator bonuses are invite only. So for now, treat those two as extra reach, not your main income.
```

```text
Here's why this works so well. It's a loop. Your Shorts get discovered by new people every single day. Some of those viewers subscribe. Your subscribers see your long-form videos and watch them. Block four is Submit and track. If you're in a Whop campaign, submit each clip link right after you post it. Then open your tracking sheet. ## v3-09-sunday
```

**The Sunday review** · 98 words · about 39 s

Save the ElevenLabs file as: `public/audio/video3/v3-09-sunday.mp3`

```text
Once a week, on Sunday, do a review. Sort your sheet by views. Look at your top five clips, and ask one question: what do these have in common? Maybe it's the same type of moment. Maybe it's the hook style. Maybe it's the time you posted. Whatever it is, make more of that next week, and do less of what's sitting at the bottom. That's how your channel gets better every single week, instead of staying exactly the same. Most people just keep posting and hoping. You'll be learning from real data, and that's a huge advantage.
```
