While all of that is building, Whop can pay you right now. Here's how it works. A creator or a brand funds a campaign on Whop, under Content Rewards. ## v1-09-risk

**The risk ladder** · 107 words · about 43 s

Save the ElevenLabs file as: `public/audio/video1/v1-09-risk.mp3`

```text
Before you clip anything, you need to know the risk ladder. Low risk: your own gameplay, Whop campaigns, and original story videos. Medium risk: creators who publicly allow clipping of their content. High risk: big creators who never gave you permission. With those, Content ID claims send the money to them, not you. You can also get copyright strikes, and three strikes deletes your channel. One more thing. YouTube's inauthentic content policy targets mass produced, repetitive, low effort videos. And reused content means posting other people's content with no meaningful changes. So always add your own touch. Stay low on the ladder, and build something that lasts.
```
