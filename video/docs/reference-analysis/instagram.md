# Instagram reference (45374573700): "Claim your business in ChatGPT" product promo

Source: 30 fps, 48.6 s (1458 frames), 16:9. All 49 grids were viewed in order (f0 to f1457).
Measurement convention: grid tiles were measured in display pixels (one tile is 333x189) and converted to 1080p with a factor of **x5.77**. Every "px" below is at 1920x1080 unless marked "tile". Durations are in seconds, with source frames (@30) in brackets. The recipes in section 4 restate every timing in **60 fps frames**.

Overall character: a quiet Apple-style product film. It uses a single off-white canvas and black headline type, and alternates two modes:
- **Chapter title cards**: a centered 1–2 line headline that is "typed" on.
- **UI walkthroughs**: flat, light UI cards that are animated as live software, with a blue cursor, hover states, scrolling, morphs and stagger.

Nothing uses gradients on the background. There are no vignettes or glows, except one aurora gradient card header and the 3D glossy icons at the open and close. The motion relies on **shared-element continuity**, **micro-staggers of 3–5 frames @30**, short **blur-fades**, and **camera moves inside UI** (scroll, zoom and push). Hard cuts are used *only* between chapter title and UI, and they always land on motion.

## 2. Animation mechanics (measured)

### 2.1 Typed headlines ("pre-laid-out reveal")
- **Layout is computed for the final string first.** Characters are revealed in place, so nothing re-centers while typing. Line 2's first glyph sits at its final x (for example, "now" at x≈600 px, already offset for the centered final line).
- **Cadence**: line 1 is usually revealed **per word** (gap 7 frames @30, about 0.23 s). Line 2 starts per word, then switches to **per character at ~1 char/frame @30 (30 cps)** with occasional 2–3 frame holds that read as human typing. "now in ChatGPT" (14 chars) takes f14–f24 (0.33 s). "info up to date" takes f259–f280. "using info you provide" takes f630–f644.
- **No fade, blur or caret** on each glyph. Glyphs pop at 100% opacity. Crispness is the style.
- **Pre-shift**: when line 2 is about to start, the block shifts up by ~85 px (half a line plus a little) over 6 frames @30, ease-out. Scene 2 does this. In other scenes the 2-line layout is static from the start.
- **Out**: always a hard cut on the next shot, except scene 14, which drifts up 60 px over 5 frames and then cuts to UI already moving up (cut on motion).
- Holds after completion: 0.6–1.6 s.

### 2.2 3D object constellation (open)
- 7 glossy 3D product icons, each ~110–200 px. Each **flies in from the nearest frame edge** (it starts 100–250 px outside the frame), with a scale of ~0.7 to 1 and 10–30° of rotation settling. Ease-out over ~0.5 s (15 frames @30). Staggered ~2–3 frames @30 by position (top ones first, bottom last).
- After landing, each keeps an ambient drift of ~1–2 px/frame and slow rotation (~0.3°/frame).
- **Implode exit**: f40–f46 (0.2 s). Every icon moves ~60–80% of the way toward the headline center while scaling 1 to ~0.2 and fading 1 to 0. Directional **motion blur ~15–25 px** along the travel vector. Ease-in. The headline is untouched.

### 2.3 Slot-machine list between headline lines
- **Gap open**: line 1 moves up ~130 px and line 2 down ~190 px over f46–f60 (0.47 s, ease-out). The gap grows ~125 to ~450 px.
- **Ticker**: rows are ~95 px pitch (tile 17), each an emoji/logo icon (~40 px) plus an UPPERCASE name (~24 px, letter-spacing ~0.05em, each brand a different saturated color: red, green, orange, blue, teal). They scroll upward **from fast to stop** over f46–f78 (~1.07 s). Velocity is ~4 rows/0.1 s at the start and decelerates exponentially, like a spin wheel.
- **Blur at speed**: vertical blur ~6–10 px while velocity is >1 row per 3 frames, then sharp.
- **Mask**: the top and bottom ~30% of the gap fade to the background (a linear-gradient mask), so rows dissolve at the edges instead of hitting the headline.
- **Collapse**: f84–f89 (0.17 s, ease-in-out). The gap shrinks to one row (~60 px). The rows above and below scroll out under the mask, leaving the chosen item ("COLORFUL CLATTER").
- Selected-item icon fades out f102–f106 and the row text re-centers horizontally (~40 px) over the same 4 frames.

### 2.4 Text-row-to-card morph (shared element)
- f108: both headline lines fade 1 to 0 over 3 frames @30 with **blur 0 to ~8 px** and a slight scale-down (~0.97).
- A card container grows from the row's bounding box. f110: width ~880 px, height ~500 px. f120: 930x875 px, top moving from y≈295 to y≈105. That is ~0.33 s, ease-out (fast start, long settle).
- The row label crossfades from "COLORFUL CLATTER" (caps, 22 px) to "Colorful Clatter" (title case, 28 px, black) over 3 frames, with ~4 px blur at the midpoint (f114).
- Hero image (top 33% of the card) opacity 0 to 1 over ~4 frames, starting while the card is still growing.

### 2.5 Card content stagger (used in ~8 places)
- Order: avatar/logo, title, subtitle, row 1, row 2, row 3, CTA.
- Each item: opacity 0 to 1 over **3–4 frames @30**, ~**8–12 px rise**, optional 4 px to 0 blur. **Stagger 3–5 frames @30** (0.1–0.17 s).
- Logo: **scale pop** 0.6 to 1.0 with a faint overshoot (circle C, ~70 px), 5 frames.
- Pale secondary text (grey `#9A9AA0`-ish) uses the same timing.
- Total for a 6-item card: ~0.6 s (f120–f138).

### 2.6 Cursor
- macOS-style arrow, **solid blue fill** (~`#2E9BE6`/`#4494C7` sampled) with a thin darker edge, ~40 px tall. No hand cursor and no click ripple.
- Entry: from just off-frame at the bottom-right (or top-right / bottom), ease-out.
  - Scene 9: travels ~450 px in the first 8 frames, then creeps ~50 px over the next 20 frames.
  - Scene 38: covers ~250 px in 6 frames.
  - The pattern is a long-tail exponential decel, so the cursor "lands" and then micro-settles.
- Hover state appears **~0.25 s after landing**. It is a 3-frame fade of a pale blue fill (`#F4F9FC`) plus a 1 px blue border (on buttons), or a pale pill behind a link row.
- Click: the target scales to ~0.97–0.98 for 2–4 frames. Dark buttons go a lighter grey (`#555` to `#888`) for ~4 frames. Then the next state.
- Click-to-transition latency: 0–3 frames. The UI responds immediately.

### 2.7 Card swap after a click (scene 10–11)
- The old card is gone in 1 frame. Its replacement is born at **scale ~0.87, opacity ~0.3, blur ~12 px** and settles to 1.0 / 1 / 0 over 9 frames @30 (0.3 s, ease-out).
- Inner text blur-fades 2 frames behind the container. List rows follow at +3 frames. The button fades in last and its fill darkens grey to black over 6 frames.
- **Badge pop**: a dot at 0 scale grows to the full scalloped seal (~110 px) with the check over 6 frames, with overshoot ~1.08. Then a **halo ring**: a white/very light ring 1.0 to 1.35x the badge diameter, opacity 0.8 to 0, over 6 frames, starting 3 frames after the badge settles.

### 2.8 Virtual scroll through a form
- 3 strokes, each a **0.3–0.5 s ease-in-out** translateY of 250–600 px, separated by 0.6–1 s holds that keep a slow drift (5–10 px/s).
- During each stroke, a **zoom of 1.00 to 1.07** (accumulating across strokes) and a slight horizontal re-centering.
- Vertical motion blur ~6–12 px only in the middle third of each stroke.
- Section cards: white, 1 px `#E8E6EC` border, radius ~24 px, 40 px padding. Section titles 30 px, labels 18–20 px, inputs grey-filled (`#F3F2F5`) with radius 12 px.
- Section entering from below during stroke 3 (f422–f438): a 16-frame ease-out (deltas in tile px per frame: 12, 15, 17, 14, 11, 9, 7, 5, 4, 3, 3, 2…). The start is ease-in-out, and it reads as "scroll then settle".

### 2.9 Modal rise
- From +150 px below its rest and opacity 0 to 1 in 5 frames, then **a long ease-out over ~1.0 s** (f481–f510) for the remaining ~50 px. It feels like a soft spring with no overshoot.
- Rows stagger **4 frames @30** apart, each a 4-frame fade with a ~6 px rise.
- The last visible row is **masked to ~30% opacity** (bottom fade) to imply more content.
- CTA pill: born at ~70% width, grey `#DDD`. Over 4 frames it goes to full width and black `#111`.

### 2.10 Internal list scroll
- About 5 rows (row pitch ~140 px), ~700 px over f520–f536 (0.53 s, ease-in-out).
- **Vertical blur ~8–12 px** on the row text at peak (f523–f530). Thumbnails blur slightly less.
- Fixed header ("Name / Price") and a fixed footer (Confirm). The content clips under a soft top/bottom fade.

### 2.11 Modal to card dissolve with flying thumbnails (shared element)
- Modal chrome and text fade 1 to 0 over 7 frames @30 (f553–f560). The hero image of the target card fades 0 to 1 over 4 frames, overlapping.
- **The 3 thumbnails** (60 px in the table) travel to the gallery slots (~140 px squares) over ~0.6–0.8 s:
  - They first **bunch into a fanned stack** (rotated −10°, 0°, +12°, offset ~25 px) as they move.
  - They then spread horizontally into the slots and straighten. Scale goes 1 to ~2.3.
  - Each item lands ~5 frames after the previous one.
- Grey placeholder squares (`#EFEDF1`, radius 16) fade in at the slots ~6 frames before the thumbnails arrive.
- The text below each tile (name, then price) staggers in 5 frames apart per tile.

### 2.12 Agent builder sections
- The whole app plate pops in on a cut and scales ~0.98 to 1.0 over 8 frames.
- Each section: header, then a paragraph that **blur-fades in (6 px to 0) over 3 frames**, staggered ~4 frames. Paragraph text appears as a block (not typed).
- **Live typing in a field**: a 2 px darker focus outline appears, then text appends at ~1 char/frame @30. A text caret is not visible at this scale.

### 2.13 Chat sequence
- Panel rise on a cut: ~140 px up in 6 frames, ease-out.
- Welcome content is staggered top to bottom at 2–3 frames per item. Chips (3) are staggered 2 frames.
- Clearing: 2-frame fade of the welcome content (a very abrupt "new conversation" feel).
- User bubble: grey `#F0EFF2`, radius ~18 px, 3-frame blur-fade (6 px to 0) at its final spot.
- **Layout push**: when a new row is added, existing content slides up by the new row's height over ~3 frames (FLIP), ease-out.
- "Thinking" **shimmer**: grey text with a lighter band (~40% of the word width) sweeping left to right, period ~15 frames @30, running ~1.1 s.
- **Streaming**: the answer appears **line by line**, each line a 2–3 frame fade with a slight blur, about 1 line per 3 frames. Product cards blur in (10 px to 0) over 4 frames, pushing the whole conversation up ~280 px with visible motion blur on the moving text.

### 2.14 Bottom sheet
- Travels ~880 px (from below the panel to y≈195) in **17 frames @30 (0.57 s)**. Deltas per frame (tile): 17, 15, 14, 14, 11, 10, 8, 5, 5, 5, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1…, which is exponential ease-out (≈ `Easing.out(Easing.exp)` or `bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- The sheet covers the panel's blue header. The underlying chat is not dimmed (no scrim).

### 2.15 Stat strip build
- An empty outlined container (1 px `#E6E4EA`, radius 20) appears at **scale 1.15**, opacity ~0.4, and settles to 1.0 / 1 over 6 frames @30.
- Columns are staggered **6 frames @30**: label fades in, then the number appears **light grey and darkens to black over 3–4 frames** (no count-up here; the value is static). A 1 px vertical divider fades in with each new column.
- Numbers: ~48 px, regular weight, tabular. Labels: ~16 px grey.
- A secondary card rises from the bottom (~150 px, 0.25 s ease-out) during the column stagger.

### 2.16 Focus shift (one element recedes, another advances)
- Over **10 frames @30 (0.33 s), ease-in-out**:
  - The strip scales 1 to 0.62 and moves up ~390 px.
  - The chart card scales 1 to 1.46 and moves up ~600 px into the center.
  - Vertical motion blur ~10–20 px on the card's text during the middle frames.
- Both stay fully opaque. It reads as a camera "dolly to the next point of interest" while the previous context stays visible.

### 2.17 Donut chart and legend count-up
- Donut: outer ~260 px, ring ~32% thickness. Colors: light blue `#3DA0F5`, dark blue `#0468C6`, grey `#E3E2E7`.
- Segments **sweep in concurrently with the whole ring rotating** (the start angle visibly moves from ~8 o'clock to 12). The total sweep is 0 to 360° over ~0.8 s (f1086–f1109), ease-out.
- Legend rows stagger **5 frames @30**. Each shows a color square, label, mentions and %. The **% counts up linearly** (0 to 45 over ~19 frames, with ease-out in the last 4 frames). Row 2 (0 to 35) starts 7 frames later, row 3 (0 to 20) 4 frames after that.

### 2.18 Horizontal push and legend reflow
- The left card slides ~600 px left and the right panel enters from x=+1920 (off-frame right), both over **~8 frames @30 core plus 4 settle** (f1133–f1146), ease-out.
- Horizontal motion blur ~10 px on the chart title.
- The left card's legend fades out (f1130–f1132), then **re-enters below the donut** (f1146–f1149) with the 3-frame stagger. This is a reflow done as fade-out, move, fade-in rather than a literal FLIP.

### 2.19 Row selection pill
- A pale-blue rounded rectangle (`#EAF3FD`) fades in behind a legend row over 3 frames. Its label turns blue `#1A7FE0` over the same 3 frames. No scale.

### 2.20 Arc conveyor of 3D icons (end card)
- Path: a parabola above the headline. Endpoints are ~±640 px from center at y≈390 (just above the headline cap height), with the apex at y≈225 and x≈+80 px (slightly right of center).
- Each icon travels ~**22 px per frame @60** (≈1300 px/s), right to left. Spacing is ~315 px, so a new icon enters every ~14 frames @60.
- The icon rotates continuously (~1.5°/frame @30) and its scale peaks at the apex (~1.1x, ~130 px tall).
- Opacity 0 to 1 over the first ~150 px of path, 1 to 0 over the last ~150 px. Entering icons also carry ~6 px blur.
- Duration on screen: ~2.3 s for the whole parade. The final traveller is a plain blue dot that **stops at apex center** (ease-out over 8 frames @30).

### 2.21 Badge coin flip, sparkle and specular sweep
- Grow: the dot grows into the seal (~170 px) over 6 frames @30.
- **rotateY**: about 540° over f1328–f1342 (14 frames @30 = 0.47 s), ease-out.
  - Width follows |cos θ| (edge-on frames show a ~10 px sliver).
  - The back face is a plain seal without the check, slightly darker.
  - Perspective is mild (~800 px).
- Sparkles: 3 four-point stars (6–14 px) at ~1 o'clock, 30–60 px out. Each scales 0 to 1 to 0 over ~8 frames, staggered 2 frames, for ~0.8 s.
- Specular: a white diagonal band (~30% of the badge width, 60% opacity) sweeps across the badge in 4 frames (f1359–f1362). A thin ring halo (1.2x diameter, opacity ~0.3) is visible for ~6 frames before it.

### 2.22 Dot to logo outro
- Headline removed by a hard cut. The badge travels from y≈230 to screen center (y=540) over 13 frames @30. It shrinks 170 to 45 px while doing another ~1.25 Y-flips, and loses the check on the back-face pass.
- Color cycle: **hard swaps every 4 frames @30 (8 @60)**, no tween. Same size, center locked. The last color holds 8 frames.
- Dot to ring "O": a hard swap. The "O" is placed at its *final* glyph position inside "OpenAI" (~180 px left of center), so the dot visibly **jumps left** to become the first letter.
- Wordmark builds in 2-frame steps, deliberately out of order: "Op", then "Op n" (the "e" missing), then "Open", "OpenA", "OpenAI". Again pre-laid-out, no re-centering. Final ~470 px wide, ~100 px cap-height type, weight ~600.

## 3. Typography, color, surfaces

- **Typeface**: a geometric/neo-grotesk sans (OpenAI Sans style; nearest open substitutes are **Inter Display**, **Geist**, **Manrope** or **General Sans**).
  - Headline: ~84–90 px, weight 500–600, tracking ≈ −0.01em, line pitch ~120 px (lh ≈ 1.35).
  - End card: ~100 px, weight 600.
  - UI text: 14–30 px regular/medium. Labels in mid-grey `#8E8C93`.
  - Small-caps brand rows: ~22–24 px uppercase, +0.05em tracking.
- **Palette**:
  - BG `#FBF9FC`. Text `#0B0B0C`. Card fill `#FFFFFF` with a 1 px `#ECEAF0` border and a very soft shadow (0 8 px 30 px rgba(30,20,60,0.06)). Input fill `#F3F2F5`.
  - Primary button fill `#111` with white 14 px label. Link blue `#2B8FE0`. Hover fill `#F4F9FC`.
  - Brand-blue header `#1E73E8` (chat). Aurora header gradient: `#7EB9E1` to `#8EBEE2` to lavender `#C9C3F0`, fading to white `#E5F6FD` at the bottom edge.
  - Product-page sheet beige `#EDE3D6` with brown CTA `#7A4E1E`.
  - Chart blues `#3DA0F5` / `#0468C6`, grey `#E3E2E7`.
- **Glow**: none on UI. The only "glow" moments are the badge halo and the specular sweep.
- **Background**: flat, no texture, no gradient. All depth comes from white cards over off-white.
- **3D assets**: glossy, softly lit product icons (basket, handbag, macarons, plant, coffee cup, paint roller, storefront, wrench, % tag, shopping bag), with no drop shadows on the BG.
- **Devices**: none. The "phone" is just a ~520x1060 px rounded panel with a blue top bar. UI is shown frameless (no browser chrome), which keeps it clean at 1080p.
- **Cursor**: blue filled arrow (described above). It never shows a hand or I-beam.
- **Rhythm**: title card ~2.0 s, then UI sequence ~4–10 s, alternating 5 times. Every UI sequence ends on a cursor click that triggers the next state.

## 4. Remotion recipes (1920x1080 @ 60 fps)

Helpers assumed below:
```ts
const ease = { outExpo: Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), inOut: Easing.bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1),
               out: Easing.bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), in: Easing.bezier(0.55, 0, 1, 0.45) };
const fade = (f, start, dur) => interpolate(f, [start, start + dur], [0, 1], {extrapolateLeft:'clamp', extrapolateRight:'clamp'});
```

### R1. Pre-laid-out typed headline (chapter cards)
- Render the full string with `visibility:hidden` per glyph (or `opacity:0`) so the layout is final from frame 0. Reveal glyph *i* at `revealFrame[i]`.
- Build the schedule:
  - **per-word** for line 1: a word every **14 frames**.
  - **per-char** for line 2: **2 frames/char** (30 cps), adding a 4–6 frame hold after every 3rd–5th char (seeded random) for a human cadence.
- No fade. Glyph opacity steps 0 to 1.
- Optional pre-shift when the block grows a line: `translateY` 0 to −85 px over **12 frames**, `ease.out`.
- Hold **40–90 frames** after completion, then hard cut. For a cut on motion, add `translateY` 0 to −60 px over 10 frames, `ease.in`, and start the next scene's main element already moving up.
- Educational use: chapter titles, key-term definitions, "Step 2: …" cards. Typing a *keyword* char-by-char after the first words were shown word-by-word gives the payoff word emphasis.

### R2. Slot-machine selector between two lines (pick one from many)
- Two text lines are absolutely positioned. Gap opening: line 1 `y` −130 px, line 2 `y` +190 px over **28 frames**, `ease.out`.
- Ticker column: rows at **95 px pitch**. Scroll offset `s(f) = D * (1 - Math.exp(-k*t))`, with D = (N−1)·95, k ≈ 4.5/s, run over **64 frames**, then snap so the target row is centered.
- Blur: `filter: blur(clamp(|v|/40, 0, 10) px)`, where v is px/frame.
- Mask: `mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, transparent 0%, #000 30%, #000 70%, transparent 100%)`.
- Collapse: gap height to 60 px over **10 frames** `ease.inOut`. Non-selected rows fade 1 to 0 over 6 frames.
- Educational use: "which of these 20 tools applies?", landing on the answer. Also good for picking a category, a framework or a variable value.

### R3. Text row morphs into a card (shared element)
- Measure the row rect. The card starts at the row rect and animates `width/height/top` to the final rect over **20 frames** with `ease.outExpo`. Use `border-radius` 12 to 28 px.
- Other text: opacity 1 to 0, blur 0 to 8 px, scale 1 to 0.97 over **6 frames**.
- Label crossfade: two stacked spans (CAPS, small vs Title case, larger), crossed over **6 frames**, with peak blur 4 px at the midpoint.
- Hero image: fade over **8 frames**, starting at frame 6 of the morph.
- Educational use: a term in a sentence expands into its definition card, or a list item expands into a detail view.

### R4. Card content stagger
- Per item: `opacity: fade(f, start + i*S, 7)`, `translateY: 10*(1-p)` px, optional `blur(4*(1-p))`. **S = 8 frames** (fast) or **10** (calm).
- Logo/avatar: `spring({fps:60, frame: f - start, config:{damping:14, stiffness:220, mass:0.6}})`, mapped to scale 0.6 to 1.
- Secondary text uses the same timing at 60% target opacity.
- Educational use: checklists, spec sheets, "what you get" lists.

### R5. Blue cursor with hover and click
- Path: position = `interpolate(f, [t0, t0+16, t0+56], [start, mid, end], {easing: ease.outExpo})`, where *mid* is 90% of the way. That gives a fast approach and a long creep (16 frames covering 90%, 40 frames for the last 10%).
- Cursor: an SVG arrow 40 px tall, fill `#2E9BE6`, stroke `#1D6FA8` 1 px, `drop-shadow(0 2px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.15))`.
- Hover: target fill `#F4F9FC` plus a 1 px `#7DB8E8` border, fading in over **6 frames**, starting **15 frames** after the cursor lands.
- Click: the target scales 1 to 0.97 to 1 over **6 frames** (3 down, 3 up). For dark buttons, `background` goes `#111` to `#666` to `#111`.
- Trigger the next state at click frame + 2.
- Educational use: any software walkthrough. It keeps the viewer's eye on the active control without a zoom.

### R6. Post-click card swap (pop-in replacement)
- Old card: unmount on the click frame.
- New card:
  - scale `interpolate(p,[0,1],[0.87,1])`, opacity 0.3 to 1, blur 12 px to 0.
  - p = `spring({config:{damping:20, stiffness:170}, durationInFrames:18})`.
- Inner stagger per R4, starting at +4 frames.
- Button fill grey to black over **12 frames**.
- Educational use: "before, then after" states, a quiz answer reveal, or a success screen after a step.

### R7. Badge pop, halo ring and specular sweep
- Pop: spring `{damping:10, stiffness:200, mass:0.7}` (overshoot ~1.08), from scale 0, settling in about 12 frames.
- Halo: a ring div at 1 to 1.35x scale and opacity 0.8 to 0 over **12 frames**, starting at +6.
- Specular: an overflow-hidden badge containing a `linear-gradient(110deg, transparent 35%, rgba(255,255,255,.65) 50%, transparent 65%)` layer translated −120% to +120% over **8 frames**.
- Sparkles: 3 SVG 4-point stars. Each scales 0 to 1 to 0 over **16 frames**, staggered 4 frames.
- Educational use: "correct!" checkmarks, completed checklist items, an "achievement unlocked" moment at the end of a module.

### R8. Coin-flip reveal (rotateY)
- Container `perspective: 800px`. Inner `transform: rotateY(θ)`, θ 0 to 540° over **28 frames**, `ease.outExpo`.
- Two faces with `backface-visibility:hidden`, the back at `rotateY(180deg)` with a darker fill and no icon.
- Combined move: translate along a path and scale 1 to 0.26 over 26 frames (R12).
- Educational use: flip a card from question to answer, or turn a concept icon into the next one ("myth, then fact").

### R9. Virtual scroll camera through a long UI
- Wrap the whole long page in one div. Animate `translateY` between keyframes, each stroke **18–30 frames** with `ease.inOut`, with holds of **36–60 frames** that keep a drift of `-0.15 px/frame`.
- Accumulate `scale` +0.025 per stroke (1.00 to 1.07 over 3 strokes), with `transform-origin: 50% 40%`.
- Motion blur: `filter: blur(${Math.min(12, Math.abs(v)/6)}px)` applied to the scroll container, only when |v| > 15 px/frame. A directional fake is better: duplicate the layer twice at ±v/3 px offsets with 0.25 opacity.
- Educational use: walking down a long form, document, code file or settings page, with each stop a teaching point.

### R10. Modal rise with long settle
- `translateY`: 150 px to 0 with `spring({config:{damping:26, stiffness:90, mass:1}})`, about 60 frames to settle, no overshoot.
- `opacity`: 0 to 1 over **10 frames**.
- Rows stagger **8 frames**. The last row is masked with `mask-image: linear-gradient(#000 75%, transparent)`.
- CTA born at `scaleX(0.7)` and `#DDD`, going to 1 and `#111` over 8 frames.
- Educational use: popovers, "preview" panels, reference tables appearing over a diagram.

### R11. Inner list fast scroll with motion blur
- Scroll the list `translateY` by about 5 rows over **32 frames** `ease.inOut`.
- Blur `min(12, |v|/5)` px on the row text layer (thumbnails at half that).
- Fixed header and footer, with top and bottom 40 px fade masks.
- Educational use: "there are hundreds of these, here's the one we want", for long logs, datasets or API lists.

### R12. Thumbnails fly from a table into a gallery (fan, then settle)
- For each item i (stagger **10 frames**), over **40 frames**:
  - position = bezier path from the table cell center to the gallery slot center, with the control point ~120 px up and toward center.
  - scale 1 to 2.3, `ease.out`.
  - rotate: 0 to fan angle (−10/0/+12°) over the first 40%, back to 0 by the end.
- Placeholders (`#EFEDF1`, radius 16) fade in 12 frames before arrival. Source modal chrome fades over **14 frames**.
- Educational use: "these inputs become that output", such as data rows turning into chart points, files into a folder, or ingredients into a recipe card. It is very strong for showing data flow without arrows.

### R13. Chat simulation (bubble, thinking shimmer, streaming, reflow)
- Bubble: blur 6 px to 0 and opacity 0 to 1 over **6 frames** at its final place.
- Layout push: every block's `translateY` follows a spring from its old y to its new y (`damping:22, stiffness:200`), so the conversation slides up as new rows arrive (FLIP).
- Thinking shimmer: text `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#9a9a9a 0%,#9a9a9a 40%,#e9e9e9 50%,#9a9a9a 60%,#9a9a9a 100%)`, `background-size:250%`, `background-clip:text`. Position cycles 100% to −100% every **30 frames**. Run for 60–70 frames.
- Streaming: split the answer into lines. Line *k* fades in over 5 frames with a 3 px to 0 blur, starting at `k*6` frames. Media cards follow with a 10 px to 0 blur over 8 frames.
- Educational use: demonstrating prompts and answers, Q&A recaps, "ask the tutor" segments.

### R14. Bottom sheet
- `translateY`: (panelHeight + 40) to 0 over **34 frames** with `ease.outExpo`. No scrim. Radius 28 on the top corners.
- Educational use: "drill into detail" from an overview, such as a footnote, definition or source citation sliding over the current scene.

### R15. Stat strip build
- Container: scale 1.15 to 1 and opacity 0.4 to 1 over **12 frames** `ease.out`, with a 1 px border `#E6E4EA`.
- Column i at `+12*i` frames:
  - label fade 6 frames.
  - number `color` interpolated `#C9C7CD` to `#0B0B0C` over 8 frames, starting 4 frames after the label.
  - divider fade.
- Static numbers (no count-up) feel calmer and suit dense explainer dashboards.
- Educational use: KPI rows, "3 numbers to remember", before/after metrics.

### R16. Focus shift (recede and advance)
- Over **20 frames** `ease.inOut`:
  - element A: `scale 1 to 0.62`, `y` −390 px.
  - element B: `scale 1 to 1.46`, `y` −600 px.
  - Both stay opaque. Blur peaks at 10 px at the midpoint (`blur = 10*sin(πp)`).
- Educational use: moving attention from a summary row to the chart it summarizes, or from an overview diagram to one node, while keeping the context visible.

### R17. Donut draw, legend count-up and selection pill
- SVG circle `stroke-dasharray`. Each segment's sweep = `share * P(f)`, with P = `interpolate(f,[s,s+48],[0,1],{easing:ease.out})`.
- Whole-ring rotation `−110deg + 110*P` (the start angle swings in).
- Legend rows stagger **10 frames**. `%` = `Math.round(target * interpolate(f,[r, r+38],[0,1],{easing: Easing.bezier(0.3,0,0.4,1)}))`, using tabular numerals.
- Selection pill: `#EAF3FD` background and label color `#1A7FE0`, fading over **6 frames**.
- Educational use: market share, time allocation, "what % of X is Y".

### R18. Horizontal push with reflow
- Left card: `x` 0 to −600 px. Right panel: `x` +1100 to 0. Both over **24 frames** `ease.outExpo`.
- Horizontal blur `min(10, |v|/8)` px on both.
- Reflow inside the left card: the old legend fades out over 6 frames *before* the move. The new below-chart legend staggers in (6-frame stagger) after it settles.
- Educational use: comparisons (left: data, right: action), cause and effect, "problem, then fix" panels.

### R19. 3D icon constellation (open) and implode (exit)
- Each icon: from an off-frame point along the outward vector (dist 300–500 px) to its rest position over **30 frames** `ease.outExpo`. Scale 0.7 to 1, rotate ±25° to rest. Stagger 4–6 frames.
- Idle: `x += sin(f/90+φ)*6`, `y += cos(f/110+φ)*6`, `rot += sin(f/120+φ)*3`.
- Implode over **12 frames** `ease.in`: position lerped 75% toward the headline center, scale 1 to 0.2, opacity 1 to 0, directional blur 20 px.
- Educational use: an opening hook around a topic word, such as tools or concepts orbiting the title before the lesson starts.

### R20. Arc conveyor (parade of concept icons)
- Path: `x(t) = 640 − 1280·t`, `y(t) = 390 − 165·(1 − ((x − 80)/720)²)`.
- Each icon has its own t, with `t = (f − f0_i)/110` (110 frames to cross) and f0 spaced **14 frames** apart.
- Opacity: `min(1, t/0.12, (1−t)/0.12)`. Rotation `t*120deg`. Scale `1 + 0.1*sin(πt)`. Blur `6*(1 − min(1, t/0.12))`.
- The final element eases to the apex over **16 frames** and becomes the hero (R7/R8).
- Educational use: "everything we covered" recap parades in outros, or "all of these feed into…" before converging on one key idea.

### R21. Dot color-cycle to wordmark
- A 45 px circle at center. Fill indexes through palette[k] with `k = floor((f − s)/8)`, a hard step (no tween). Hold the last color for 16 frames.
- Then swap the dot for the first glyph of a pre-laid-out word (the dot jumps to the glyph's x).
- Reveal the remaining glyphs in **4-frame** steps, in a slightly scrambled order (e.g. [0,1,3,2,4,5]).
- Educational use: course or brand sign-off, section bumper, "one idea, many facets" (each color is a module color).
