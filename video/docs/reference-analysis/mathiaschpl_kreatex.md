# Supplementary motion references: mathiaschpl (Weespy V2 promo) + KreateX27914 (Base44 promo)

How this was measured: I viewed all 37 grids for mathiaschpl and all 10 for KreateX, every frame, at 30 fps sampling (one tile = 33.3 ms = 2 frames at 60 fps).
- Pixel values are converted to 1080p. One tile is about 330x188 px in the displayed grid, so the factor is about 5.75.
- Durations come from counting frames: "N tiles" equals N/30 s, which is 2N frames at 60 fps.
- Blur amounts are estimated by eye from edge softness. Treat them as ±30%.

### 2. Animation mechanics (measured)

**A. Odometer counter, $1K → $300K (0.37–1.30 s)**
- Entry: the "$1K MRR" glyphs rise from about +60 px below with vertical blur of about 10 px. They stagger left→right about 1 frame (30 fps) apart and take about 0.13 s each.
- Each digit is its own rolling column. The next digit slides up from below with heavy vertical motion blur, and ghosted neighbouring digits are visible mid-roll.
- Value trace at 30 fps: 1, 2, 5, 13, 14, 25, 26, 36, 37, 48, 149, 159, 156, 161, 262, 273, 274, 282, 285, 296, 297, 298, 298, 299, 300.
  - It covers 91% of the range in the first 0.53 s, then crawls for the last 0.3 s.
  - **Duration ≈0.83 s (50 f @60). Ease-out (expo/quint-like). No overshoot.**
- Comet line: starts as a dot at the left edge (0 s), sweeps a rising arc to the top-right corner (1.0–1.3 s), and stays as a 2 px white curve at about 70% opacity.

**B. Word-typewriter with an accent fade (the signature text treatment, used in 7 headlines)**
- Words appear one at a time, about **0.13–0.17 s apart (8–10 f @60)**.
- Each new word fades in over 2–3 frames at 30 fps (≈0.08 s, **5 f @60**) from a blur of about 4 px, with no translate.
- The word **arrives in accent red (#E8343F) and settles to the base colour** (white on hills, #1A1A1A on light) about 0.2–0.3 s later, with a 0.1–0.15 s colour crossfade. This happens to every word, not only keywords. Key nouns ("competitor", "wondering", "click") stay red longer, about 0.4 s.
- Block caret (≈0.5 em × 1.15 em, same colour as the text) sits right after the last glyph. It stays solid while typing. When idle it blinks with a period of about 0.9–1.0 s, fading over about 2 frames at 30 fps rather than hard-cutting.
- Mono variant ("zero answers"): **character by character at about 1 char per 2 f @60**. Each character arrives red and then turns white.
- The search field uses a thin red I-beam instead of a block. Characters arrive at 50% grey and darken over 2 frames. **Rate ≈30 chars/s.**

**C. Inline object inside a sentence (3.60–3.70 s)**
- A capsule (≈330x95 px, white with a 1 px light border and 6 px drop shadow) pops in **in the text flow**.
- Mechanics: blur 10 → 0 and scale 0.85 → 1 over 3 frames at 30 fps (**6 f @60**). The caret is pushed right.
- The thumbnails inside scroll horizontally, about 80 px per 0.3 s, looping.

**D. Element-to-element morph with camera zoom (5.00–5.23 s, 14 f @60)**
- The headline text scales to about 1.15, moves up 20 px, blurs to about 10 px, and fades to 0 over 0.1 s.
- The capsule stretches in X (scaleX about 3) with horizontal motion blur of about 20 px over 0.07 s. It then scales up about 4x toward the camera and slides to the left edge over 0.13 s with an ease-out, landing as the search bar. There is no visible overshoot.

**E. Camera tracking a caret (5.4–6.5 s)**
- The world translates left so the caret stays at about x≈55–85%. Pan speed is about 600 px/s, smooth and constant.
- The "Search" button enters from the right edge with the pan, as a world-space element rather than its own animation.

**F. Zoom-out reveal (6.53–6.97 s, 26 f @60)**
- Scale about 2.3 → 1.25 (≈ ×0.55), with an ease-in-out speed profile.
- Directional/zoom blur on text of about 6–8 px peaks in the middle third and is gone at the end.
- Afterward the camera drifts slowly, about 1% scale per second.

**G. Chip stagger (7.03–7.47 s)**
- Four chips are spaced about 0.13 s apart (8 f @60).
- Each fades 0 → 1 over 0.1 s with a 4 px blur and an x offset of about -10 px. There is no reflow because the slots were already reserved.

**H. Cursor**
- macOS black arrow at about 34 px.
- Moves in a straight line or slight arc over 0.35–0.65 s with an ease-out (fast start).
- Click feedback: **red ring**, radius about 30 → 55 px, 2 px stroke, opacity 0.8 → 0, over 0.13–0.2 s. On the extension icon it adds a red glow (box-shadow about 30 px) plus 2–3 thin rings expanding to about 1.8x the icon size over about 0.5 s.

**I. 3D card stream (8.0–9.6 s)**
- About 12 webpage cards, each ≈330x230 px, arranged in a diagonal staircase receding toward the top-right with perspective. Front cards are larger and blurrier from depth of field.
- The whole stack translates along its diagonal at about 250 px/s. Motion blur on entry is about 15 px.
- Tilt is approximately rotateX 10–20° and rotateZ about -8°.

**J. White bloom wipe (9.20–9.70 s, 30 f @60)**
- A radial white gradient grows from the right-centre (origin about x 85%, y 35%): radius 0 → 150% of the frame diagonal, ease-in.
- Under it, the content blurs from 0 to about 20 px and fades. The new scene's text then blurs in at 9.7.

**K. Vertical wheel / slot picker (10.0–11.6 s)**
- Pitch is about 150 px. The selected slot sits in a white pill (≈720x130 px, radius 999, shadow 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.08)).
- Opacity falloff: about 1.0 for the selected icon, 0.55 at ±1 slot, 0.3 at ±2, 0.12 at ±3. Icons above and below the selection are also blurred about 2 px per slot.
- Each step takes about 3 frames at 30 fps (**6 f @60**), with vertical motion blur of about 10 px on the step frames. The label crossfades as the old one slides up and out and the new one slides up and in, about 15 px.
- Rhythm: dwell 0.55 s, 0.33, 0.2, 0.13, then a fast spin of 3 slots in 0.17 s (heavy blur), then lands on Weespy with a small settle. This is the classic **slot-machine** decelerate-to-land.

**L. Logo descent and shockwave (12.0–13.5 s)**
- The logo translates from y 19% to y 50% (about 335 px) over 1.27 s with an ease-in-out. Rotation wobbles between -6° and +4°, and the logo bobs about ±8 px.
- The red underglow ellipse grows in opacity from 0 to 0.6 as the logo nears the "ground".
- Shockwave: 4 elliptical rings of red halftone dots (dot about 6 px, aspect about 3:1, flattened by perspective). Radius grows from the logo size to about 1100 px over about 0.35 s. Spacing between rings is about 60 ms, and each ring fades out over its last 40%. Lingering arcs stay at about 30% for 1 s.

**M. Pill grow-out (13.47–13.6 s)**
- Width grows from the logo square (≈190 px) to about 780 px over 0.13 s with an ease-out. The pill is anchored left at the logo.
- Text types at 1 char per 2 f @60. The "V2" badge appears with scale 0.6 → 1 and opacity over 0.1 s.

**N. Blur-dissolve exit and entry (used for every scene-to-scene handoff)**
- Exit: blur 0 → 12–16 px, opacity 1 → 0, scale 1 → 1.04, over **0.13–0.2 s (8–12 f @60)**.
- Entry (browser 15.3–15.47): blur ~20 → 0, opacity 0 → 1, scale 1.12 → 1.0, over **0.2 s (12 f)** with an ease-out. Exit and entry overlap by about 2 frames.

**O. Punch-in then pull-back (16.6 / 17.07–18.4 s)**
- Hard cut into a 4x close-up, then a long ease-in-out zoom back to 1x over about 1.3 s.
- The popup panel grows downward out of the icon (scaleY / translateY about -40 px, opacity) over about 0.4 s while the camera pulls back.

**P. Card lift-out (19.40–19.80 s, 24 f @60)**
- The card scales 1 → 2.2 and translates from its position in the popup to the frame centre. The ease-out has a long tail.
- The browser behind blurs from 0 to about 20 px and fades from 1 to 0 over the same window.

**Q. Counter with synchronized line-chart draw (19.87–20.37 s, 30 f @60)**
- Mono digits in red while counting, turning #111 when done.
- Linear for 70% of the time (+3.4M per 33 ms), then decelerating over the last 4 frames (13.6 → … → 39.0 → 39.9 → 40.6).
- The line draws left→right in lockstep with the counter. A glowing head dot (≈10 px with a 16 px pink glow) rides the tip.

- The tooltip pill (white, radius 999, shadow) appears **simultaneously**: opacity 0 → 1, x -20 → 0, blur 6 → 0, over 0.13 s.
- The flying number docks into the tooltip at its red-bold position.
- The source row gets a pink highlight bar (rgba(232,52,63,.12), radius 6) that fades in over 0.1 s.

**S. Card-to-card morph (21.87, 23.87, 25.83, 27.80 s; 0.17–0.2 s each)**

### 3. Typography, colour, glow, background, UI

- **Display numerals:** condensed high-contrast serif *italic* (Instrument Serif Italic or PP Editorial New Ultralight Italic family), white with no shadow. About 250 px cap height on 1080p with tight tracking (-2%).
- **Headlines:** geometric/neo-grotesk sans (Inter Display / SF Pro / Geist), weight 600, 44–56 px, tracking -1.5%. White on the hills scenes, #1A1A1A on light scenes.
- **Mono accent line:** Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono, regular, same size, wider tracking.
- **Colours:**
  - Accent red #E5303A (brand red #E3262E)
  - Pink chart #E0466B; area fill rgba(224,70,107,.18 → 0)
  - Green delta #1FA463
  - Off-white UI background #F3F4F6
  - Card white #FFFFFF with 1 px border rgba(0,0,0,.06) and shadow `0 12px 40px rgba(20,20,40,.10)`
  - Dark button #1E1E1F
- **Glow:** red brand objects carry a coloured under-glow, `0 16px 40px rgba(229,38,46,.45)` plus a soft halo. The chart head dot has a pink glow of 12–16 px. White text on the hills has no glow.
- **Background:** one continuous camera move over a painted 3D landscape, used in three states:
  - **sharp** for hero moments
  - **blur about 8 px** under text
  - **blur about 30 px + 50–60% white wash** under UI cards
  - Changing the background blur is itself the transition device, a rack focus.
- **UI mockups:** flat, light dashboard cards (radius about 28 px, 1 px border, soft shadow), mini chips with brand logos inline in titles, "NEW" red pill badges (≈48x24 px), and tooltip pills with a red dot bullet and red bold numbers.
- **Cursor:** macOS black arrow throughout.

### 2. Animation mechanics

- **Push-in on a word:** scale 1.0 → 1.10 over 0.4 s, linear-ish.
- **Orb zoom-through:** a circular mask centred on the orb, radius from about 45 px to the frame diagonal (≈2200 px) in about 0.07 s, ease-in. It cuts on the beat. A smoother Remotion version runs over 8–10 f @60.
- **Split-and-insert (1.23–1.57 s, 20 f @60):**
  - The words translate outward ±(pill width/2 + 20 px) with horizontal motion blur of about 8 px.
  - Pill width grows 0 → 640 px with an ease-out (a fast start, then a long settle over the last 40%).
  - The words crossfade with blur (old blur 0 → 6 px; new 6 → 0) during the widening.
  - The send icon fades in at 80% progress.
- **Auto-growing text box (2.0–2.4 s, 24 f @60):** height follows the wrapped text. Growth is smoothed by an ease-out with no overshoot, anchored at the centre.
- **Hard-cut montage:** 20 f @60 per shot, no transition. Each card keeps a slow drift (about 1% scale per second). Continuity comes from keeping the card frame and position constant.
- **Flash and colour wipe:** a background exposure flash brightens to near-white over 5 f and decays over 5 f. The wordmark colour changes through a left→right gradient wipe with a soft edge of about 60 px, over 0.2–0.3 s.
- **Card slide-in:** y +85 px → 0 over 0.33 s (20 f @60) with an ease-out. The card appears at full opacity on its first frame because it is cut in.
- **Halftone dissolve and radial reveal (≈0.95 s, 56 f @60):**
  - Phase 1 (≈14 f): a dot grid fades in as a wave travelling bottom→top. Each dot's radius grows 0 → pitch × 0.48 as the wave passes, coloured by the underlying pixel (pastel source plus teal/green from the incoming image).
  - Phase 2 (≈6 f): the UI card dissolves into the dot field.
  - Phase 3 (≈36 f): a circular reveal from the centre. Inside radius r the target image is clean. Across a band of about 120 px at the edge, dot size gradates from full to zero (a halftone feathered edge). r grows from 0 to the frame diagonal with an ease-in-out.
  - During Phase 3 the frame insets to a rounded card: margin 0 → 45 px, radius 0 → 40 px.
- **UI overlay assembly (7.53–7.8 s):**
  - The panel slides x -40 → 0 and fades, with title lines revealing behind a mask left→right.
  - Pins pop with scale 0 → 1.1 → 1, staggered about 0.05 s (3 f @60).
  - The dashed route draws as a stroke-dashoffset over about 0.3 s.
  - The lime card slides up 20 px and fades.
- **Reflow to an editor layout (9.1–9.5 s, 24 f @60):** the preview scales to 0.84 and translates x +6% with an ease-out. Chat lines fade in staggered about 0.03 s apart.

### 3. Typography, colour, background

- Sans (Helvetica Now / Inter) in regular and medium weights only. It is very airy with small text sizes. Dark #2A2A2A on white, and white on the mesh.
- Gradient mesh palette: orange #F4935F, peach #F7B38D, lemon #F5EE9A, lavender #D8D1EE, blush #F1C9CF, cream #EFE8D2. Rendered as 3–4 large blurred radial blobs (blur ≥150 px) that drift slowly.
- Cards: white, radius about 24–30 px, shadow `0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`. The send button is a dark circle.
- Mockups: full-bleed product screenshots, each on a background matching its palette. Accent lime #DDF46B on the travel app.

## 4. Remotion recipes (1920x1080, 60 fps)

Shared helpers. `ease` maps to `Easing.bezier(...)`.
```ts
const OUT = Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);      // expo-out: most entries
const INOUT = Easing.bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1);   // camera moves, morphs
const IN = Easing.bezier(0.55, 0, 1, 0.45);      // exits / fly-aways
const SPRING_SOFT = { damping: 20, stiffness: 170, mass: 1 };   // no visible overshoot
const SPRING_POP  = { damping: 12, stiffness: 220, mass: 0.8 }; // ~6% overshoot, pins/badges
```

### R1. Word typewriter with accent settle (bullet text and headlines)
- Words start at `i * 9` f (≈0.15 s). For mono or char mode use `i * 2` f per character.
- Per word: opacity 0 → 1 and blur 4 → 0 px over **5 f** with `OUT`, and no translate.
- Colour: `interpolateColors(f - start, [10, 22], [ACCENT, BASE])`. Keywords hold red for about 24 f before settling, or stay red.
- Block caret: `width: .5em; height: 1.15em; background: currentColor`. It is solid while `f < lastWordStart + 10`. Idle blink: `opacity = 0.5 + 0.5 * cos(2π * f / 56)`, clamped to a hard 0/1 with 4 f fades.
- Educational use: bullet lists where each bullet's key term lands red then settles. It also works as the caption system for every scene.

### R2. Odometer number counter (big stats)
- Value: `interpolate(f, [0, 50], [from, to], { easing: Easing.bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) })`.
- Render each digit as a vertical strip of 0–9 inside an `overflow: hidden` slot of height 1 em. Set `translateY = -digitFloat * 1em`, where `digitFloat` is the continuous digit value so slots roll through intermediate positions.
- Fake vertical motion blur: `filter: blur(${min(8, |dDigit/df| * 6)}px)` with `transform-origin` centre, or SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 N"`.
- Entry: glyphs rise `translateY 60 → 0` with blur 10 → 0, staggered 2 f per glyph over 8 f each, using `OUT`.
- Dashboard variant (R2b): use a mono font. The value is linear for 70% of the duration, then decelerates: `Easing.bezier(0.3, 0.6, 0.2, 1)` over 30 f. It shows red while counting and turns #111 at the end. The delta chip ("+26.2%") pops in 4 f after the end with `SPRING_POP`.

### R3. Line-chart draw synced to the counter
- `pathLength` progress p equals the counter progress, from the same `interpolate` call. Use `strokeDasharray = L` and `strokeDashoffset = L * (1 - p)`.
- Head dot: `getPointAtLength(p * L)`. Radius 5 px with `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 8px #E0466B)`.
- Area fill: a gradient path with opacity `interpolate(f, [end, end + 6], [0, 1])`, starting only after the line is complete.

### R4. Data-fly to a tooltip (call-out an insight)
- At f0 a duplicate value element, absolutely positioned, travels from the source rect to the target rect over **15 f** with `INOUT`.
- Its path is a quadratic arc: `y += -sin(πp) * 40px`. Scale 1 → 0.9.
- The tooltip pill enters at f0: opacity 0 → 1, x -20 → 0, blur 6 → 0, over 8 f with `OUT`. At p = 1 the flying copy hides and the tooltip's own number shows.
- Source row highlight: background `rgba(232,52,63,.12)` with `scaleX 0 → 1` from the left over 6 f.

### R5. Blur-dissolve scene transition (default cut, 10–12 f)
- Exit: opacity 1 → 0, blur 0 → 14 px, scale 1 → 1.04, over 10 f with `IN`.
- Entry: opacity 0 → 1, blur 18 → 0, scale 1.10 → 1.0, over 12 f with `OUT`, starting 2 f before the exit ends.
- Pair it with a background rack focus: `bgBlur` interpolates between states 0 / 8 / 30 px over the same 12–18 f.

### R6. Card-to-card morph (topic → topic inside one "panel")
- Keep one container `div`. Interpolate its width and height between card sizes over **12 f** with `INOUT`.
- Old content: opacity 1 → 0 and blur 0 → 12 over frames 0–7. New content: blur 10 → 0 and opacity 0 → 1 over frames 5–12.
- The title fades out (4 f) before the morph and the new title types in (R1) starting at frame 10.

### R7. Card lift-out / focus zoom (zoom into one part of a diagram)
- Target scale 1 → 2.2 and translate to the frame centre over **24 f** with `OUT`.
- The rest of the layer: blur 0 → 20 px and opacity 1 → 0 over the same 24 f.
- Reverse it for a "zoom back out to the whole diagram" beat.

### R8. Camera punch-in and pull-back (detail → context)
- Wrap the scene in a "camera" div with `transform: scale(s) translate(x, y)`.
- Punch-in: hard cut at s = 4 on the detail.
- Pull-back: s 4 → 1 over **80 f** with `INOUT`, while child UI (a popup panel) slides down 40 px and fades over 24 f.
- Caret-tracking pan: `x = -max(0, caretX - 0.7 * W)` smoothed with `spring({ damping: 30, stiffness: 120 })`.

### R9. Staggered chips, legend rows, checklist items
- Stagger: **8 f** for chips and **3 f** for legend or table rows.
- Each item: opacity 0 → 1, blur 4 → 0, x -10 → 0, over 6–8 f with `OUT`.
- Checklist variant: the row's tick circle uses `SPRING_POP` scale 0 → 1. Its check path draws over 8 f (stroke-dashoffset). The label's colour runs red → base as in R1.

### R10. Bars, donut and progress bars
- Bars: `scaleY 0 → 1` from the bottom, 12 f each, stagger 4 f, `OUT`. Highlight the key bar in solid accent and draw the others in accent at 30%.
- Donut: SVG circle with `strokeDasharray` per segment. Total sweep progress runs 0 → 1 over **18 f** with `OUT`, and the segments fill sequentially. The centre counter follows R2b.
- Horizontal progress bars: `scaleX` from the left, 12 f, stagger 4 f.
- Sync callout words to the sections: highlight word i at the moment section i starts animating (12 f stagger).

### R11. Hub-and-spoke / mind-map build (flow charts, concept maps)
- Nodes start at `30 + i * 10` f, in clockwise order.
- Node position: `lerp(hub, final, 0.6 → 1.0)` over 6 f with `OUT`. Blur 8 → 0 and opacity 0 → 1.
- Connector: a cubic Bézier from the hub edge to the node, stroke 1.5–2 px in the accent colour. Draw it over **8 f** starting 2 f before the node lands. Add an arrowhead marker at the hub end (arrows point into the hub, meaning "data flows in").
- Flow dots: 2–3 circles of radius 3 per edge at `getPointAtLength(((f / 30 + k / 3) % 1) * L)`, travelling node→hub every 30 f.
- Node values count up (R2b) over 30 f.
- Hub pulse: a ring with `scale 1 → 1.35` and opacity 0.6 → 0 over 15 f, repeated twice 10 f apart.
- Collapse: each node lerps to the hub over 10 f with `IN`, plus a 10 px blur and fade.
- Linear flow-chart variant: the same mechanics, but each node appears to the right of the previous one, the connector draws first (8 f), then the node lands (6 f), on a 12 f rhythm.

### R12. Slot-machine / wheel selector (choosing among options)
- List of items with pitch P = 150 px. `offset = spring/interpolate` per step.
- Step durations in frames: [33 dwell, 6 move], [20, 6], [12, 6], [8, 6], then a 10 f multi-slot spin, then a 12 f final settle with `SPRING_SOFT`.
- Per item: `opacity = [1, .55, .3, .12][|d|]` and `blur = 2 * |d| px`, where d is the distance in slots from the selection.
- Vertical motion blur during moves: SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="0 ${speed * 0.6}"`.
- The label inside the selector pill crossfades with y ±15 px over 6 f.

### R13. Inline object in a sentence
- Reserve its width in the text flow. Pop it in with blur 10 → 0, scale 0.85 → 1 and opacity over **6 f** using `OUT` (not a spring). The next words then continue typing.

### R14. Shockwave / ripple emphasis (punctuating a key moment)
- 4 SVG ellipses (rx : ry ≈ 3 : 1 for a ground plane, or 1 : 1 flat), stroked with a dot pattern (`strokeDasharray="0 12"`, `strokeLinecap="round"`, stroke-width 6) in the accent colour.
- Ring k starts at 4k f. Its radius goes from the logo radius to 1100 px over **21 f** with `OUT`. Opacity is 1 for the first 60%, then falls to 0.
- Click variant (UI): one or two 2 px rings, radius 30 → 55 px, opacity 0.8 → 0, over 8–12 f.

### R15. White bloom wipe (topic/section change)
- An overlay radial gradient `radial-gradient(circle at 85% 35%, #F4F4F7 0%, #F4F4F7 R%, transparent R+25%)` with R 0 → 160 over **30 f** using `IN`.
- The underlying content blurs 0 → 20 px over the same span. The new scene's text blur-enters (R5) at frame 30.

### R16. Halftone radial reveal (KreateX; "aha"/result reveals)
- Render on a `<canvas>` (or with `@remotion/three`). For each grid cell (pitch 38 px), set dot radius = `pitch * 0.48 * mask(cell)`, coloured with the sampled source image.
- Phase 1 (14 f): `mask = smoothstep(waveY - 120, waveY, cellY)`, with waveY travelling from H+120 to -120.
- Phase 3 (36 f, `INOUT`): draw the target image clipped to a circle of radius r. In the band between r and r + 120 px, draw target-coloured dots with radius `pitch * 0.48 * (1 - (dist - r) / 120)`.
- At the same time, inset the frame: margin 0 → 45 px, borderRadius 0 → 40 px.

### R17. Split-and-insert (KreateX): turn a phrase into an input/box
- The words translate ±(w/2 + 20) over 20 f with `OUT`, with horizontal blur peaking at 8 px mid-move.
- Pill width 0 → w over 20 f with `OUT`. The words crossfade to new words with a 6 px blur.
- Growing box: height follows a target measured from the text, smoothed with `spring({ damping: 26, stiffness: 140 })` so it never jumps.

### R18. Hard-cut montage in a fixed frame (examples / "it can build anything")
- Shots of **20 f** each in the same card rect. Each shot gets a 1.00 → 1.02 scale drift and a background that matches its palette. Exit the last shot with opacity → 0.4 over 4 f, then cut.

### R19. Orb / letter zoom-through (title → scene)
- Mask the next scene with `clipPath: circle(r at orbX orbY)`, r 45 → 2300 px over **8–10 f** with `IN`. Before it, push the title in with scale 1 → 1.1 over 24 f.

### R20. Background system (shared)
- One continuous slow camera layer, scale 1.0 → 1.08 plus x drift of about 1.5% per 10 s, used in three states:
  - hero: blur 0
  - text: blur 8 px
  - UI: blur 30 px + `rgba(244,244,247,.55)` wash
- Transition between states over 15–20 f with `INOUT`. This rack focus does much of the scene-change work by itself.
